# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** QP-Prostate® CAD (K242683)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Standalone: 247; Clinical reader: 228 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, multiple centers across the US |
| Baseline Comparison | QP-Prostate® CAD outputs were compared to ground truth diagnoses derived from associated pathology reports and radiologist interpretations |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, race/ethnicity / Not reported: sex/gender, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: AUC-ROC, Sensitivity, False Positive Rate per Case, AUCunaided, AUCaided, DAUC |
| Women's Health | No, the study relates to prostate cancer, which predominantly affects men |
