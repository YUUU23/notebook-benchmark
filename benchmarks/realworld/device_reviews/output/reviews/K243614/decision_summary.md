# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Sonio Suspect (K243614)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: Not reported; Test: 287 |
| Samples (N) | Training: Not reported; Test: 750 images |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, 47 sites including 37 in the United States |
| Baseline Comparison | The primary objective of this study was to determine whether the performance of readers assisted by Sonio Suspect ('Assisted') was superior to the performance of readers when not assisted by Sonio Suspect ('Unassisted'). |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: Race, Ethnicity, BMI, Gestational Age (GA), Maternal age / Not reported: Age, Sex/Gender, Comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: AUCDelta, AUCUnassisted, AUCAssisted |
| Women's Health | Yes, the study relates to fetal ultrasound findings which are relevant to pregnancy and obstetrics. |
