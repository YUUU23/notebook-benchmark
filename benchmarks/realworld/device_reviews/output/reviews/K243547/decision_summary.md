# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** uMR Ultra (K243547)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 108; Validation: 60; AiCo Training: 114; AiCo Test: 24; SparkCo Training: 10; SparkCo Test: 15; ImageGuard Test: 80; EasyCrop Test: 65; EasyFACT Test: 25; Auto TI Scout Test: 27; Inline MOCO Test: 60 |
| Samples (N) | AiCo Training: 140,000 images; AiCo Test: 218 samples; SparkCo Training: 24,866 spark slices; SparkCo Test: 59 cases; ImageGuard Test: 191 cases |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). Each volunteer was scanned by UIH MRI systems for multiple body parts and clinical protocols, resulting in a total of 140,000 images. |
| Multi-site | Yes, various clinical sites (specific sites not named) |
| Baseline Comparison | Comparing the proposed device and predicate device. 'Sample clinical images for all clinical sequences and coils were reviewed by three U.S. board-certified radiologists comparing the proposed device and predicate device.' |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, race/ethnicity, BMI / Not reported: comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, Bland-Altman analysis showed less than 1% bias and all sample points fall in the 95% confidence interval. |
| Women's Health | No — The study involves general MRI imaging techniques and does not specifically relate to women's health. |
