# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** AIR Recon DL (K252379)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Clinical Image Quality Study: 34 datasets (patients not specified); Quantitative Post Processing: 6 volunteers |
| Samples (N) | Clinical Image Quality Study: 34 datasets; Quantitative Post Processing: Multiple sequences repeated 4 times per volunteer |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). 'Each sequence was repeated 4 times and retrospectively reconstructed to generate images with product reconstruction and AIR Recon DL with Phase Correction.' |
| Multi-site | Not reported |
| Baseline Comparison | Predicate device. 'The DLPC and the PC-ARDL models have undergone bench testing with in-vivo data to evaluate the feature and its impact on image quality relative to the predicate device.' |
| Demographics Disclosed | Not reported: age, sex/gender, race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Not reported |
| Women's Health | No — the device is intended for use with all anatomies and patients of all ages, not specifically related to women's health. |
