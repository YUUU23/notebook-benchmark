# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Better Diagnostics Caries Assist (BDCA) Version 1.0 (K241725)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Standalone: 614 BW images, 684 IOPA images (patients not specified); MRMC: 328 digital radiographs (patients not specified) |
| Samples (N) | Standalone: 614 BW images, 684 IOPA images; MRMC: 328 digital radiographs |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). 'A patient had at most one BW and one IOPA image included in the analysis dataset... multiple cavities (surfaces) within an image might lead to potential positive correlations.' |
| Multi-site | Not reported |
| Baseline Comparison | Ground truth was determined through the consensus of two out of three experienced, licensed dentists. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Not reported |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, CIs reported for BW Surface Level Sensitivity, BW Surface Level Specificity, IOPA Surface Level Sensitivity, IOPA Surface Level Specificity, BW Image Level Sensitivity, IOPA Image Level Sensitivity |
| Women's Health | No — The device is intended for identifying carious lesions in dental radiographs for patients aged 18 years or older, which is not specific to women's health. |
