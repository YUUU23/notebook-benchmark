# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Assert-IQ  (DM5000); Assert-IQ (DM5300); Assert-IQ (DM5500); Merlin.net (MN7000) (K251221)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | CARE-Pause: 1498; CARE-AF: 911; Assert-IQ post-market study: 151 |
| Samples (N) | Not reported |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). 'To minimize the risk of bias caused by frequent similar episodes, a maximum of two episodes were randomly selected from each patient during the available monitoring period.' |
| Multi-site | Yes, CARE-Pause: 504 clinics across the United States; CARE-AF: 360 clinics across the United States |
| Baseline Comparison | Holter monitor. 'The overall system performance of Assert IQ with CARE-AF is assessed using data collected from the Assert-IQ post-market study (NCT06172699) comparing device detection against a Holter monitor.' |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: sex/gender, age / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Not reported |
| Women's Health | No — the device is for cardiac monitoring and arrhythmia detection, which is not specific to women's health. |
