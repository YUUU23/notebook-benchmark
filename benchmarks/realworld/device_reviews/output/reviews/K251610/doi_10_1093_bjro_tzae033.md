## Clinical Context

The tool assists in identifying potential large vessel occlusions on CT angiogram images of the brain in the acute setting, notifying a neurovascular specialist for further review. It is a triage aid, operating parallel to standard diagnostic workflows.

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective, using head CTA scans, time period not specified

## Metrics

- Sensitivity: 91.35% (95% CI: 87.54%-94.07%)
- Specificity: 91.86% (95% CI: 88.18%-94.47%)
- AUC: 0.959 (95% CI: 0.943 – 0.975)
- Mean Time to Notification: 6.36 minutes (95% CI: 6.06-6.66)
