## Clinical Context

The tool addresses triage and preliminary notification of suspected large vessel occlusion (LVO) in CT angiogram images of the brain, specifically in the internal carotid artery (ICA) and M1 segment of the middle cerebral artery (MCA).

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective study of head CTA scans for LVO presence, evaluated by US board-certified neuroradiologists.

## Metrics

Sensitivity: 91.35% (95% CI: 87.54%-94.07%), Specificity: 91.86% (95% CI: 88.18%-94.47%), AUC: 0.959 (95% CI: 0.943–0.975), Mean Time to Notification: 6.36 minutes.
