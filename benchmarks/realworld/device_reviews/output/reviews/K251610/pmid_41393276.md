## Clinical Context

The qER-CTA tool addresses the triage of suspected large vessel occlusions (LVOs) in the brain by notifying neurovascular specialists. It is a notification-only tool that works parallel to the standard of care to prioritize images for review.

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective study using 584 head CTA scans, including 289 LVO cases and 295 non-LVO cases, with ground truth established by neuroradiologists.

## Metrics

Sensitivity: 91.35% (95% CI: 87.54%-94.07%), Specificity: 91.86% (95% CI: 88.18%-94.47%), AUC: 0.959 (95% CI: 0.943–0.975), Mean Time to Notification: 6.36 mins (SD not provided)
