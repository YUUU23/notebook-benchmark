## Clinical Context

The tool is intended as a notification-only, parallel workflow tool to identify CTA brain images with possible large vessel occlusion (LVO) and notify a specialist for review, operating independently of the standard of care.

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective study on patients undergoing CTA scans for suspected large vessel occlusion within a multicenter setting.

## Metrics

- Sensitivity: 91.35% (95% CI: 87.54%-94.07%)
- Specificity: 91.86% (95% CI: 88.18%-94.47%)
- AUC: 0.959 (95% CI: 0.943 - 0.975)
