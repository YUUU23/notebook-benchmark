## Clinical Context

qER-CTA is a notification-only, parallel workflow tool used to identify and communicate images of patients with suspected large vessel occlusion to specialists, aiding in triage and prioritization within hospital networks.

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective study with a sample consisting of head CTA scans, with ground truthing performed by three US board-certified neuroradiologists.

## Metrics

Sensitivity: 91.35% (95% CI: 87.54%-94.07%), Specificity: 91.86% (95% CI: 88.18%-94.47%), AUC: 0.959 (95% CI: 0.943 – 0.975), Mean Time to Notification: 6.36 minutes [6.06, 6.66].
