# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** MuscleView 2.0 (K251682)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 1,658; Validation: 1,294; Training: 1,192; Validation: 374 |
| Samples (N) | Training: 1,658 scans; Test: 460 scans |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, 19 – 22 new sites |
| Baseline Comparison | Performance testing of segmentation and associated metrics was performed by comparing the results obtained to a reference standard developed by manual segmentation performed by experts. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, race/ethnicity / Not reported: comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: DICE (DSC), absolute volume difference |
| Women's Health | No — the device is for general musculoskeletal imaging and does not specifically relate to women's health. |
