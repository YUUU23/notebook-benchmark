# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** CHLOE BLAST (K243851)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | 703 embryos from 59 mothers |
| Samples (N) | 703 embryos |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, 3 sites in the United States |
| Baseline Comparison | The study evaluated the performance of the CHLOE BLAST algorithm (Odds Ratio and other measures) in the prediction of blastocyst formation using embryo assessment conducted based on Day 3 morphology alone, and on Day 3 morphology with CHLOE score as adjunct information. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, BMI / Not reported: sex/gender, race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: AUC of the overall performance of the Blast prediction |
| Women's Health | Yes, the study relates to assisted reproduction and embryo assessment, which are relevant to women's health. |
