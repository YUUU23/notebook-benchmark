# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Second Opinion® CS (K243234)

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Standalone: 1250; MRMC: 330 |
| Samples (N) | Standalone: 1250 radiograph images; MRMC: 330 radiographs |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, multiple geographical regions in the United States |
| Baseline Comparison | Standalone CAD performance compared to unaided readers and equivalence to the predicate, Overjet Caries Assist (K222746). |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: wAFROC-FOM difference, lesion-level sensitivity, false positives per image (FPPI), surface-level specificity |
| Women's Health | No — the study relates to caries detection in dental radiographs, which is not specific to women's health. |
