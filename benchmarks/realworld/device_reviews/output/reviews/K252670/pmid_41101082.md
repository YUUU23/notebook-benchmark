## Clinical Context

Alzevita is intended for use by neurologists and radiologists for automated labelling, visualization, and volumetric measurement of the hippocampus from brain MRI scans. It assists in the evaluation of neurodegenerative conditions by comparing hippocampal volumes to a normative dataset.

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective study using data from the Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) dataset, covering data from May 2024 to July 2025.

## Metrics

Dice Score: 86% (95% CI: 85-86%), Hausdorff Distance: 1.51 mm (95% CI: 1.43-1.59 mm), Correlation Coefficient: ≥ 0.82, Relative Volume Difference: ≤ 24.6%, Bland-Altman Mean Difference: ≤ 1010 mm³.
