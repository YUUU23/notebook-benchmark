## Clinical Context

Alzevita automates the labelling, visualization, and volumetric measurement of the hippocampus from high-resolution T1-weighted MRI images. It is intended to assist neurologists and radiologists in comparing hippocampal volumes against a normative dataset as part of diagnostic workflows.

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective study using subjects from the publicly available ADNI dataset and training data from MRI scans collected in India from May 2024 to July 2025.

## Metrics

Dice coefficient: 0.86 (95% CI: 0.85-0.86), Hausdorff Distance: 1.51 mm (95% CI: 1.43-1.59 mm)
