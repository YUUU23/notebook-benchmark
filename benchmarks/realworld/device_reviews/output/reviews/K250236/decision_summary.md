# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Swoop® Portable MR Imaging® System (V2) (K250236)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Model/Sequence group: T1, T2, FLAIR: 40; Model/Sequence group: DWI: 29; Advanced Reconstruction Image Validation: 32; Contrast-to-Noise Ratio Validation: 15 |
| Samples (N) | Model/Sequence group: T1, T2, FLAIR: 111 images; Model/Sequence group: DWI: 94 images; Advanced Reconstruction Image Validation: 167 images; Contrast-to-Noise Ratio Validation: 46 images |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). "Pathologies spanning multiple slices were used multiple times." |
| Multi-site | Yes, 4 sites for T1, T2, FLAIR and DWI; 3 sites for Advanced Reconstruction Image Validation; 2 sites for Contrast-to-Noise Ratio Validation |
| Baseline Comparison | Linear Reconstruction was used as the reference standard for the comparison. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | No, confidence intervals are not mentioned for the primary performance metrics. |
| Women's Health | No, the device is for imaging the internal structure of the head and is not specific to women's health. |
