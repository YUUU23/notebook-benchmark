# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** DEEPECHO (K242594)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: Not reported; Test: 431 (Femur Length), 858 (Head Circumference), 499 (Abdominal Circumference), 858 (Biparietal Diameter) |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). Each subject contributed multiple images, including standard fetal biometry planes and non-standard views. |
| Multi-site | Yes, training data from two Roshan MFM clinics in New York City and selected sites in Morocco; test data from Seattle (WA), Garden City (NY), Union (NJ), Honesdale (PA), Chiapas (Mexico), and Casablanca (Morocco). |
| Baseline Comparison | Ground truth measurements: 'Comparison of DEEPECHO to ground truth measurements' |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, race/ethnicity / Not reported: sex/gender, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: Femur Length, Head Circumference, Abdominal Circumference, Biparietal Diameter, Sensitivity and Specificity of view identification |
| Women's Health | Yes, the study involves fetal measurements and anatomical view identification, which are relevant to obstetrics and women's health. |
