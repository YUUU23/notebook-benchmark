# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Withings ECG App (K240795)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 11,701; Test: 5089; Clinical Trial: 626 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, multi-center clinical trial |
| Baseline Comparison | Rhythm classification of a 12-lead ECG by a cardiologist was compared to the rhythm classification of a simultaneously collected ECG from the Withings ECG App. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, comorbidities / Not reported: race/ethnicity |
| Confidence Intervals | No, confidence intervals not reported for sensitivity and specificity metrics |
| Women's Health | No — The device is an ECG app intended for general use in detecting atrial fibrillation and is not specific to women's health. |
