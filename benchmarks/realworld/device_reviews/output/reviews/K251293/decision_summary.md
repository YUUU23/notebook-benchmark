# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** CardioVision (K251293)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | 608 |
| Samples (N) | 650 echocardiography studies |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, 12 independent clinical sites across the United States |
| Baseline Comparison | The combination of high sensitivity and specificity reinforces its substantial equivalence to the predicate device. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, weight, BMI / Not reported: sex/gender, race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: Sensitivity, Specificity, PPV, NPV |
| Women's Health | No — the device is intended for detecting aortic stenosis, which is not specific to women's health. |
