# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Viz Subdural+, Viz SUBDURAL PLUS (K250354)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Subdural collection measurement performance (volume and thickness) and midline shift were assessed on datasets with 203 and 151 cases, respectively. |
| Samples (N) | Not reported |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, two clinical sites |
| Baseline Comparison | Device results for subdural collection volume, widest width and midline shift were also compared to the truther consensus by Bland-Altman plots and linear regression analysis. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Not reported: age, sex/gender, race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and DICE Score have 95% Confidence Intervals |
| Women's Health | No — the device is intended for automatic labeling, visualization and quantification of collections in the subdural space from Non-Contrast Head CT images, which is not specific to women's health. |
