# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Allix5 (DEN240047)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Pivotal: 77,511; Reproducibility Study #1: 1,046; Reproducibility Study #2: 102 |
| Samples (N) | Pivotal: 77,511 exams; Reproducibility Study #1: 1020 transformed exams; Reproducibility Study #2: 2400 total exams |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). Quote: 'A random selection of 10 transforms were applied to each of the 102 test exams, yielding 1020 transformed exams for analysis.' |
| Multi-site | Yes, 27 sites (Training: 22 sites; Validation: 5 sites: Emory University Hospital Midtown, Solis Mammography at Baylor Scott & White All Saints Medical Center, Solis Mammography Bedford at Lisa Trent Breast Center, University of California, Davis Health Ellison Ambulatory Care Center, University of California, Davis Folsom Clinic) |
| Baseline Comparison | No comparator. Quote: 'The current standard of care uses traditional mathematical risk models such as the Gail Model and Tyrer-Cuzick Model.' |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, race/ethnicity, breast density, breast implants / Not reported: sex/gender, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, AUC(5): 0.70 with 95% confidence interval (0.69, 0.72) |
| Women's Health | Yes, the device is intended to generate a 5-year risk prediction of breast cancer based on a bilateral screening mammogram. |
