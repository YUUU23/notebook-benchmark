# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** OsteoSight Hip (v1) (K251408)

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | 3082 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, six independent cohorts from the US (including the Midwest, Northeast, South, and West) |
| Baseline Comparison | sensitivity and specificity of the device with the accepted gold standard, Dual-Energy X-ray Absorptiometry (DXA) |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, BMI, race / Not reported: comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, sensitivity and specificity have CIs |
| Women's Health | Yes, the study involves bone mineral density, which is a significant concern in women's health, particularly post-menopausal women. |
