# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** MammoScreen® (4) (K243679)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | 1,475 |
| Samples (N) | 2,950 (each patient underwent a DBT acquisition with two Hologic mammography systems) |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, number of sites not specified |
| Baseline Comparison | The objective of these MRMC studies was to determine whether the radiologist's performance when using MammoScreen is superior to unaided radiologist performance for interpretation of mammograms. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: Race, Ethnicity / Not reported: Age, Sex/Gender, Comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: AUC ROC (exam level), AUC ROC (breast level), AUC LROC (finding level) |
| Women's Health | Yes, the device is intended for use in interpreting mammograms, which are specific to breast health. |
