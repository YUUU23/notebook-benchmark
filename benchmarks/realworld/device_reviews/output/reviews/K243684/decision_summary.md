# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** BrightHeart View Classifier (K243684)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | 579 |
| Samples (N) | 2290 clinically acquired images and frames from video clips |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, 8 centers |
| Baseline Comparison | The reference standard was derived from the dataset through a truthing process in which a sonographer and an MFM specialist with experience in fetal echocardiography determined the presence or absence of standard views on fetal ultrasound images. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: sex/gender, age, ethnicity/country / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, mean standard view recognition sensitivity and specificity |
| Women's Health | Yes — relates to fetal heart ultrasound scanning and gestational age examinations |
