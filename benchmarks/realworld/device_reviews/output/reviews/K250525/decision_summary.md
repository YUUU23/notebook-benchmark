# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Second Opinion® Panoramic (K250525)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Impacted Teeth: 420; Periapical Radiolucency: 496; Caries: 511 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, geographic distribution across Northwest, Northeast, South, West, Midwest regions in the United States |
| Baseline Comparison | The test dataset includes a diverse range of conditions aligned with the device’s intended use, including primary and secondary caries, as well as cysts, bone loss and caries present within or around an impaction. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs include lesion level wAFROC differences and lesion-level sensitivity gains |
| Women's Health | No — the device is intended for dental radiograph analysis, which is not specific to women's health. |
