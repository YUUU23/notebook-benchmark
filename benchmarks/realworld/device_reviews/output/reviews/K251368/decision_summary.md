# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Completeness: 750; Measure: 441 |
| Samples (N) | Completeness: 5,554 images; Measure: 6,024 images |
| Patients vs. Samples | No — dependence correctly handled (e.g. mixed models, per-patient aggregation, clustered standard errors). Confidence intervals (CI) has been derived using bootstrap resampling at the patient level. By sampling patient files and simulating intra-patient correlation, the bootstrap methodology appropriately accounts for potential image correlation. |
| Multi-site | Yes, 6 sites in the United States and France |
| Baseline Comparison | No comparator |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, race/ethnicity, BMI, gestational age / Not reported: sex/gender, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: sensitivity, specificity, limits of agreement |
| Women's Health | Yes — The device is intended for fetal ultrasound examinations, which are related to pregnancy and obstetrics. |
