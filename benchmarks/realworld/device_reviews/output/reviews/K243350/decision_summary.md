# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Rapid Neuro3D (K243350)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Segmentation quality study: 115; Segmentation accuracy study: 48 |
| Samples (N) | Segmentation quality study: 120 CTA cases; Segmentation accuracy study: 50 CTA cases |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). The segmentation quality study used 120 CTA cases from 115 patients, and the segmentation accuracy study used 50 CTA cases from 48 patients. |
| Multi-site | Not reported |
| Baseline Comparison | Quote: 'The primary endpoint, clinical accuracy, as determined by the consensus of up to three clinical experts against the source DICOM images, passed for all RN3D outputs.' |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Not reported |
| Women's Health | No — the device is related to neurovascular imaging and is indicated for adults without specific focus on women's health. |
