# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Accuro 3S (K243937)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 147; Test: 81 |
| Samples (N) | Training: 25,536 images; Test: 10,080 image frames |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, 7 geographically diverse sites |
| Baseline Comparison | Ground truth for anatomical labels were established from a panel of three board certified radiologists. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, ethnicity / Not reported: comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Not reported |
| Women's Health | No — the device is for general musculoskeletal imaging and neuraxial anesthesia guidance, not specific to women's health. |
