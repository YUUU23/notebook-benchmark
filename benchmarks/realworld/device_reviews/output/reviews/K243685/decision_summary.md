# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** MammoScreen BD (K243685)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 32,368; Hologic: 922; GE: 500 |
| Samples (N) | Training: 108,775 studies; Hologic: 1,155 studies; GE: 500 studies |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, 2 different US sites for training data |
| Baseline Comparison | The reference standard for breast density value was established by majority rule among the assessment of 5 breast radiologists with at least 10 years of experience in breast imaging interpretation. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, race/ethnicity / Not reported: sex/gender, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, Kappa quadratic for Hologic and GE |
| Women's Health | Yes, the device is intended for breast density assessment in women undergoing screening mammography |
