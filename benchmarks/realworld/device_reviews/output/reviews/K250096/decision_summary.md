# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Felix NeuroAI  System (K250096)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Felix group: 83; Sham group: 42 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, 12 sites (8 in the US, 4 in China) |
| Baseline Comparison | Felix was shown to be superior to sham in change in TETRAS mADL score from baseline pre-stimulation to 90 days. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, BMI, duration of essential tremor, prior treatment for ET, use of anti-tremor and antidepressant medications / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, for TETRAS mADL score differences |
| Women's Health | No — the device is indicated for aiding tremor-related functional limitations in the upper limbs in adults with essential tremor, which is not specific to women's health. |
