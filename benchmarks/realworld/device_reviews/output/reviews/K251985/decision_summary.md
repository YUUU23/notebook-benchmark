# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** LOGIQ E10 (K251985)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 582; Confirmatory US: 15; Confirmatory EU: 24 |
| Samples (N) | 1186 annotation images |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, multiple sites in Japan, US, and EU |
| Baseline Comparison | The UGFF index is based on a least squares fit (estimation) between the acoustic property measurements and the corresponding MRI Proton Density Fat Fraction (MRI-PDFF %) measurements. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, BMI, race/ethnicity / Not reported: comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: Porta Hepatis measurement accuracy, average absolute error, Limits of Agreement |
| Women's Health | No, the study relates to general liver measurements and not specifically to women's health. |
