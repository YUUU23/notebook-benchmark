# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** CT VScore+ (K252217)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Development: 210; Validation: 236 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, data from six U.S. states across four U.S. regions and multiple institution types |
| Baseline Comparison | Consensus manual scoring on an FDA-cleared device (Vitrea CT VScore, K243240) and a 2+1 consensus process by three U.S. board-certified radiologists/cardiologists. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: Total Agatston Score ICC(2,1), Total Volume Score ICC(2,1), CAC-DRS 4-Class Kappa, CAC Standard 5-Class Kappa |
| Women's Health | No, the device is intended for non-invasive evaluation of calcified lesions of the coronary arteries based on ECG-gated, non-contrast cardiac CT images for patients aged 30 years or older, which is not specific to women's health. |
