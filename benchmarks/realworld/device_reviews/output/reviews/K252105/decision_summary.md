# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Ligence Heart (K252105)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | 524 |
| Samples (N) | 600 echocardiograms |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | No, single-site at a U.S. based independent Echocardiography Core Laboratory |
| Baseline Comparison | Agreement between Ligence Heart and three independent expert sonographers was quantified using the reference-scaled IEC. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, IEC 95% confidence interval |
| Women's Health | No, the device is for general adult cardiac ultrasound analysis. |
