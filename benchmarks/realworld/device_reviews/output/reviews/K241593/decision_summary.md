# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Adults: 190; Children: 50; Adolescents: 161 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Not reported |
| Baseline Comparison | The clinical standalone performance study compared the performance of BoneMetrics US in the measurements of Cobb angles against a ground truth by computing the Mean Absolute Error (MAE) and an acceptance criteria. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age / Not reported: sex/gender, race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
