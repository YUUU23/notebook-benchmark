# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Nelli (Version 7.11) (K251506)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Phase I: 104; Phase II: 74 |
| Samples (N) | Same as patients |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, 3 sites: TJU, MLH, USF in the United States |
| Baseline Comparison | Measured against an expert panel’s review of video EEG (the current gold standard for seizure monitoring) |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age / Not reported: sex/gender, race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, metrics with CIs: Sensitivity, False Detection Rate |
| Women's Health | No — the device is for seizure monitoring in adults aged 18 and above, not specific to women's health |
