# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** AutoDensity (K251078)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 4,679; Test: 129; Clinical precision: 30; Clinical agreement: 253 |
| Samples (N) | 9,358 images (training); Same as patients (testing and clinical studies) |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | Yes, multiple sites (US and France) |
| Baseline Comparison | Bland-Altman agreement between AutoDensity and the reference device BMD measurements |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, BMI / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Yes, for AutoDensity precision CV% |
| Women's Health | No — the study relates to bone mineral density estimation for orthopedic pre-surgical assessment, not specifically women's health. |
