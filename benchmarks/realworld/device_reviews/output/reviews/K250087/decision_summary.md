# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Vscan Air (K250087)

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training/Validation: 159; Verification: 142 |
| Samples (N) | Training/Validation: 2,197 images; Verification: 1,817 images |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent). Each individual was scanned in two views: Transverse and Longitudinal. |
| Multi-site | Yes, 5 countries: USA, Germany, UK, Japan, India |
| Baseline Comparison | Not reported |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, race/ethnicity / Not reported: comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | No, only a 90% confidence level for automatic caliper placement success rate is mentioned |
| Women's Health | Yes, the device includes applications for fetal/obstetrics and gynecological exams |
