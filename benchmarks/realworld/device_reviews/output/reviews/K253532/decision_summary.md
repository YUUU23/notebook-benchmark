# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** TruSPECT Processing Station (K253532)

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | Training: 328; Test: 24 |
| Samples (N) | Training: 1,014 images; Test: 74 images |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |

| Baseline Comparison | The reference standard was the clinical routine high-count SPECT image (~1.0 MCounts) acquired under standard D-SPECT protocols. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, BMI / Not reported: race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Not reported |
| Women's Health | No — the study relates to myocardial perfusion SPECT noise-reduction performance, which is not specific to women's health. |
