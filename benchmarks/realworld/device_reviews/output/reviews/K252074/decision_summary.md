# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** Aplio i900, Aplio i800 and Aplio i700 Software V9.0 Diagnostic Ultrasound System (K252074)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | 30 patients |
| Samples (N) | Minimum 294 images |
| Patients vs. Samples | Yes — pseudoreplication risk (multiple observations per patient treated as independent) |
| Multi-site | No, single-site (U.S. clinical site) |
| Baseline Comparison | Images with 3-HI were compared to images without 3-HI (predicate functionality) using a 5-point ordinal scale. |
| Demographics Disclosed | Disclosed: age, sex/gender, race/ethnicity / Not reported: comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | Not reported |
| Women's Health | No — the device is used for a wide range of clinical applications, not specifically focused on women's health. |
