## Clinical Context

The AiORTA - Plan tool is an image analysis software used as a measurement tool for volumetric assessment in the clinical decision-making process for the assessment of aortic aneurysm. It integrates into workflows as an adjunct tool for pre-operative evaluation, planning and sizing for cardiovascular intervention and surgery, and post-operative evaluation.

## Experimental Set-up

Retrospective study using CT data from both US and Canadian clinical centers. Exact study time period is not specified.
