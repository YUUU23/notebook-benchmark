## Clinical Context

The AiORTA-Plan tool aids in the assessment of aortic aneurysms, facilitating pre-operative evaluation, planning and sizing for cardiovascular intervention and surgery as well as post-operative evaluation. It integrates as an adjunct measurement tool for healthcare practitioners.

## Experimental Set-up

The setup for validation involves retrospective data analysis using clinical cases from US and Canadian centers, verified against ground truth annotations from certified radiologists.
