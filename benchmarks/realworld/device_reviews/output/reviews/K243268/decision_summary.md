# FDA Decision Summary: Study Characteristics

**Device:** TipTraQ (TTQ001) (K243268)  

---

## Study Characteristics

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---|
| Patients (N) | SpO2 validation: 12; Sleep validation: 147 |
| Samples (N) | Sleep validation: 147 samples |
| Patients vs. Samples | N/A — one observation per patient |
| Multi-site | Yes, 2 sites: University of California San Francisco (UCSF) and Duke University Hospital |
| Baseline Comparison | SpO2 validation study compared the TipTraQ sensor performance to reference arterial blood gas sampling; Sleep validation study compared the performance of the TipTraQ device to the gold standard in-lab polysomnography (PSG). |
| Demographics Disclosed | Not reported: age, sex/gender, race/ethnicity, comorbidities |
| Confidence Intervals | No, confidence intervals not reported for primary performance metrics |
| Women's Health | No — the device is intended for sleep apnea evaluation/diagnosis, which is not specific to women's health. |
