Title: First Clinical Use of a Novel AI-Based Imaging Tool to Enhance CTO PCI Planning
Authors: Saleh, Khatri
Journal: JACC: Case Reports
Year: 2026
PMID: 41342808
DOI: 10.1016/j.jaccas.2025.106311
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41342808/

Abstract:
This case report describes the first clinical application of AngioWave's AI-based spatiotemporal enhancement processing software, which identified previously unrecognized pathways in chronic total occlusion percutaneous coronary intervention, aiding in procedural success.
