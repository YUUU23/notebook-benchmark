Title: Retrospective audit of an artificial intelligence software for the detection of intracranial haemorrhage used by a teleradiology company in the United Kingdom
Authors: Vimalesvaran, Fu, Novak, Robert, Kumar, Tanamala, Oke, Bhatia, Shah, Romsauerova, Das, Espinosa, Grzeda, Narbone, Dharmadhikari, Harrison, Gooch, Woznitza, Salik, Campbell, Khan, Lowe, Shuaib, Ather

Abstract:
This retrospective evaluation assesses the performance of Qure.ai's qER software in detecting intracranial haemorrhages in a teleradiology setting.
