Title: Towards human-AI collaboration in radiology: a multidimensional evaluation of the acceptability of AI for chest radiograph analysis in supporting pulmonary tuberculosis diagnosis
Authors: Engle, Gabrielian, Long, Hurt, Rosenthal
Journal: PLOS One
Year: 2020
PMID: 31978149
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0224445
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31978149/

Abstract:
This study evaluates the technical performance, usability, and health impact of implementing Qure.ai's qXR AI system to support radiologists in diagnosing pulmonary tuberculosis. It includes a diagnostic accuracy evaluation and a qualitative human factors assessment.
