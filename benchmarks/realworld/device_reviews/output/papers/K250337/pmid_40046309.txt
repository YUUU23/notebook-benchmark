Title: Abdominal aortic aneurysm classification based on dynamic intraluminal thrombus analysis during cardiac cycle
Authors: Guest, Forneris, Satriano, Moore, Di Martino
Journal: J Vasc Surg Cases Innov Tech
Year: 2025
PMID: 40046309
DOI: 10.1016/j.jvscit.2025.101736
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40046309/

Abstract:
This research introduces a classification system for abdominal aortic aneurysms (AAAs) based on the dynamic analysis of intraluminal thrombus (ILT) during the cardiac cycle. The study emphasizes the importance of ILT dynamics in assessing AAA stability and rupture risk, proposing that dynamic ILT behavior could serve as a predictive marker for clinical outcomes.
