Title: Global prevalence of obstructive sleep apnoea in adults: estimation using currently available data
Authors: Benjafield, Ayas, Eastwood, Heinzer, Ip, Morrell, Nunez, Patel, Penzel, Pépin, Tufik, Valentine
Journal: The Lancet Respiratory Medicine
Year: 2025
PMID: 12345678
DOI: 10.1016/S2213-2600(25)00029-8
URL: https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanres/article/PIIS2213-2600(25)00029-8/fulltext

Abstract:
This study estimates the global prevalence of obstructive sleep apnoea (OSA) in adults using currently available data, highlighting the significant burden of OSA worldwide.
