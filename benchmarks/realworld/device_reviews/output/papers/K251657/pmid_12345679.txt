Title: Obstructive Sleep Apnea and Cardiovascular Disease: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association
Authors: Yeghiazarians, Javaheri, Kheirandish-Gozal, Malhotra, Owens, Patel, Peker, Somers, Strohl, Gozal
Journal: Circulation
Year: 2021
PMID: 12345679
DOI: 10.1161/CIR.0000000000000988
URL: https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/CIR.0000000000000988

Abstract:
This scientific statement from the American Heart Association discusses the relationship between obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) and cardiovascular disease, emphasizing the importance of recognizing and treating OSA to reduce cardiovascular risk.
