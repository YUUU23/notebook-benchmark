Title: Signa SP/2 – A MRI System for Image Guided Surgery
Authors: Author(s) not specified
Journal: Medical Laser Application
Year: 2002
PMID: N/A
DOI: 10.1078/1615-1615-00053
URL: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1615161504700538

Abstract:
This article introduces the Signa SP/2, a 'double-doughnut' magnet MRI system developed by GE Medical Systems. Designed for image-guided, computer-assisted minimally invasive surgical procedures, the system integrates a tracking/navigational system to guide instruments such as biopsy needles, laser fibers, and cryo probes.
