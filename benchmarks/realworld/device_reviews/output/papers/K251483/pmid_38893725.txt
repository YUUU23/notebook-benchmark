Title: Deep Learning-Based High-Resolution Magnetic Resonance Angiography (MRA) Generation Model for 4D Time-Resolved Angiography with Interleaved Stochastic Trajectories (TWIST) MRA in Fast Stroke Imaging

Abstract:
This study investigated the application of SwiftMR in enhancing the quality and diagnostic performance of 4D time-resolved, contrast-enhanced MRA for ischemic stroke imaging. The results demonstrated that SwiftMR significantly improves image quality and diagnostic confidence in stroke imaging.
