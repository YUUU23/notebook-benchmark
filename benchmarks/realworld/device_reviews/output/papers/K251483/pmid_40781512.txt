Title: Deep learning-based image enhancement for improved black blood imaging in brain metastasis
Authors: Oh, Paik, Jo, Choi, Yoo, Choi
Journal: European Radiology
Year: 2025
PMID: 40781512
DOI: 10.1007/s00330-025-11920-7
URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40781512/

Abstract:
This study evaluates the utility of deep learning-based image enhancement for improving the image quality and diagnostic performance of 3D contrast-enhanced T1-weighted black blood MR imaging for brain metastases. The authors find that deep learning-based image enhancement improves image quality and sensitivity for detecting small brain metastases.
